Track Your Shipping Container Online

Tracking your shipping container online is the fastest and most convenient way to stay updated with real-time location, shipment status, and estimated delivery timelines. Whether you are an importer, exporter, freight forwarder, or logistics company, having access to accurate container tracking information helps improve planning, reduces delays, and ensures complete visibility throughout the shipping journey. At Container Bazar, we simplify container tracking by allowing users to enter a container number, booking reference, or bill of lading details to instantly retrieve the latest updates from major global shipping carriers. Our system delivers streamlined tracking results that help users stay informed and manage shipments more efficiently, with accurate vessel schedules, port updates, and live movement data. With our tool, customers can monitor containers from the moment they are dispatched to their arrival at the final destination, making it easier than ever to track logistics operations effectively.

Enter Container Number & Carrier Details

To begin tracking, simply enter your container number into the search field. A standard container number consists of four letters followed by seven digits, uniquely identifying your shipment across global ports and carrier systems. Providing the carrier name—such as Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd, or ONE—helps enhance the accuracy of the search results and ensures that you receive the most up-to-date tracking details. When available, adding the shipping line information allows our system to pull data directly from the carrier’s official tracking database, giving you real-time updates including gate movements, vessel loading status, port departures, and customs release information. This step is essential for businesses that handle multiple containers or shipments across different lines, ensuring reliability and time-saving efficiency for logistics planning.

How to Track Your Container Shipment

Tracking your container shipment involves a simple process: enter your container number or booking details, select the carrier (optional), and review the tracking results generated by our system. Once the information is submitted, the system scans carrier databases and vessel AIS tracking to provide the latest status updates. These updates often include departure port, transshipment port, current vessel position, voyage number, and the expected arrival date at the destination port. This ensures full visibility across the entire supply chain. The tool is especially beneficial for importers and exporters who need to anticipate delivery windows, manage warehouse operations, coordinate onward transportation, or communicate shipment progress to customers. With continuous updates, you can avoid delays, manage unforeseen disruptions, and take timely actions based on accurate shipping information.

View Container Tracking Results & Status

Once you submit your shipment details, our online tracking tool displays detailed results including current container location, last movement event, loading or unloading updates, vessel name, and estimated arrival date. These insights allow you to monitor shipment progress in real-time and track any changes in transit caused by weather conditions, port congestion, customs inspections, or vessel delays. With accurate tracking data, you can plan ahead and communicate more effectively with customers, suppliers, or transportation partners.

Provide Container Number and Shipping Line

Providing the correct container number and shipping line helps the system connect with the exact carrier database, ensuring accurate tracking results. Each container number uniquely identifies your shipment, while carrier information ensures the search is aligned with the correct operational records.

Add Booking Reference or Bill of Lading Details

If you do not have the container number, adding the booking reference or bill of lading number is an effective alternative. Carriers use these details to track entire shipments even before containers are assigned or during multi-container bookings.

Estimated Arrival Date & Current Location Updates

Your tracking results will also provide an estimated arrival date based on vessel schedules and port operations. Real-time location updates help you understand your container’s journey, anticipate delays, and make informed logistics decisions. These insights allow businesses to streamline operations, reduce downtime, and plan delivery arrangements in advance.
